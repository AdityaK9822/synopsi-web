HIST212 — Rome's Republic, lecture 9 — FROM PRINCIPATE TO EMPIRE (CAESAR, WARS, AUGUSTUS)

1. political context (towards 1st century BC)
   - First Triumvirate: Pompey, Crassus, Caesar — informal alliance against optimates in the Senate.
   - Caesar's consulship (59 BC) led to Gallic campaign (58-51). Crosses the Rubicon in 49 BC — "the die is cast."
   - civil war → later victory at Pharsalus.
   - Dictator perpetuo (44 BC) → assassination on Ides of March.
2. post-assassination chaos & Second Triumvirate
   - Octavian, Antony, Lepidus (43 BC); proscriptions worsen.
3. Battle of Actium (31 BC)
   - Octavian vs Antony & Cleopatra; Egypt becomes province after.
4. Augustus' Principate begins (~27 BC)
   - Senate grants wide spectrum of command mixed with republican forms.
   - out-of-rome military and civic integration; propaganda (Ara Pacis).
5. assessing Augustus:
   - "restored republic" rhetoric vs concentrated autocratic powers.
   - absent robust succession; Pax Romana commercial growth expands.
   - civil strife after Varus (AD 9) stressed replacement.

Exam drill: define First vs Second Triumvirate; explain Caesar crossing Rubicon context; compare Republic vs Principate; interpret Actium.
